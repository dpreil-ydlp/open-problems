## Statement (verbatim)

```text
For what functions $g(N)\to \infty$ is it true that\[\lvert A\cap \{1,\ldots,N\}\rvert \gg \frac{N^{1/2}}{g(N)}\]implies $\limsup 1_A\ast 1_A(n)=\infty$?
```

## Recent literature

“Erdős Problem 40”; “Erdős–Turán representation function threshold”; “Sidon set density g(N)”; queried arXiv, Google Scholar (2023–2026), and MathOverflow.

- **arXiv (2026-05-29): [An Improvement of Konstantoulas’ Density Constant](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30922).** Improves density-based lower bounds for pair-sum representation counts. The result is related to the threshold question, but does not characterize all functions g(N) or establish unboundedness in the full generality asked.
- **Google Scholar (2026-08-27): [On the Erdős-Turán Additive Bases Conjecture](https://maths-proofs.edounze.com/pdfs/erdos_turan.pdf).** Presents a logarithmic-order additive-basis construction and states that the Erdős–Turán conjecture remains open; it is background, not a resolution of this threshold problem.
