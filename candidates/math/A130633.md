# A130633: For n > 15, a(n) > 1.

- **Source:** [OEIS A130633](https://oeis.org/A130633)
- **Date accessed:** 2026-10-15
- **Proposer:** Charles R. Greathouse IV (conjecture, Feb 12 2025)
- **Area:** Number theory; Fibonacci numbers and decimal digit sums
- **Tractability score:** 2/5

## Statement

> For n > 15, a(n) > 1.

## Context

a(n) is the additive persistence of the nth Fibonacci number: the number of repeated decimal digit-sum operations needed to reach one digit.

## Numerical evidence

OEIS gives terms for n=0..86. Charles R. Greathouse IV reported checking through n=10^10 on Feb 12 2025; the conjecture and range are recorded in the entry.

Saved terms: [A130633.terms.txt](A130633.terms.txt) (87 indexed terms, first 200 maximum). Source b-file: https://oeis.org/A130633/b130633.txt.

## Why it may be tractable

The sequence is generated by a familiar recurrence, and the target is a simple lower bound on decimal digit-sum persistence.

## Limits and cautions

A lower bound on the digit sum of every sufficiently late Fibonacci number can be much harder than its simple statement suggests.
