## Statement

> This is a permutation of the natural numbers.

## Context

a(1)=1, a(2)=2; for n>1, a(n) is the smallest positive integer not used earlier that shares a factor with a(n-2)+a(n-1).

## Numerical evidence

## Why it may be tractable

The rule is deterministic and local. A proof might track which prime factors are introduced and show that no positive integer is permanently skipped.

## Limits and cautions

Greedy permutation problems often require global control over prime-factor availability; not an obvious evening proof.
