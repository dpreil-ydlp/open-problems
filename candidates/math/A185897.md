# A185897: For n > 1, a(n) is odd when n = 3*2^k through 4*2^k - 1 for k >= 0.

## Statement

> For n > 1, a(n) is odd when n = 3*2^k through 4*2^k - 1 for k >= 0.

## Context

The sequence is defined by the generating function A(x - A(x)^2) = x/(1-x), where A(x)=sum_{n>=1} a(n)x^n.

## Why it may be tractable

The claim is a precise parity pattern on dyadic intervals. The implicit generating-function equation may simplify after reduction modulo 2.

## Limits and cautions

The sequence grows rapidly, so the evidence is only computable terms; the formula has a formal-power-series layer.
