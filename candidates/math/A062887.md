## Statement

> The only numbers n for which a(n)=0 are those for which 2*n+1 is divisible by 125.

## Context

a(n) is the smallest multiple of 2n+1 whose digits are odd and each digit is two less (mod 10) than the previous digit; it is 0 if no such number exists.

## Why it may be tractable
