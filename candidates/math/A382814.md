# A382814: a(n) = n/2 if and only if n is in {2, 8, 10, 32}.

- **Proposer:** Peter Kagey (sequence and conjecture, Apr 05 2025)
- **Area:** Combinatorics; greedy game and Fibonacci numbers
- **Tractability score:** 4/5

## Statement

> a(n) = n/2 if and only if n is in {2, 8, 10, 32}.

## Context

a(n) counts the nachos received by the first player in the Fibonachos game, in which players take Fibonacci-number portions in order and restart the Fibonacci list when the remaining pile is too small for the next portion.

## Numerical evidence

OEIS gives a table for n=1..70. Peter Kagey added the sequence and conjectures Apr 05 2025. The listed terms show equality at n=2,8,10,32 in the displayed range.

## Why it may be tractable

The equality target is a short finite classification in a deterministic game. Fibonacci greedy decompositions may give a compact invariant or a direct bound.

## Limits and cautions

The table ends at 70. This does not establish that no later equality occurs.
