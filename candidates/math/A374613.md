# A374613: infinitude of primes with a neighboring-prime condition

- **Source:** [OEIS A374613](https://oeis.org/A374613)
- **Date accessed:** 2026-10-15
- **Proposer:** No separate conjecture credit in the entry; sequence author Marcin Barylski (Jul 14 2024). The entry also links Stefano Spezia’s enumeration code (Jul 15 2024)
- **Area:** Elementary number theory; primes and consecutive primes
- **Tractability score:** 1/5

## Statement

> This sequence is infinite.

The sequence consists of primes p for which neither 2(p+q)-1 nor 2(p+q)+1 is prime, where q is the next prime after p.

## Why it may be tractable

The two composite tests are explicit and involve only a prime and its successor. A local congruence pattern might rule out primality in a systematic set of cases.

## Limits and cautions

This is a weak evening candidate. The claim requires infinitely many primes satisfying a condition on consecutive-prime gaps and two related numbers. That may be beyond elementary methods. The score is 1/5.
